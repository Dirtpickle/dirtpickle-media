CORRUPTED FAMILY — FULL MIDI CONCEPT ALBUM DRAFT

Concept:
A dark theatrical album about a person gradually realizing that the hostile
voice he believes belongs to the world is also coming from inside him.

The songs share a family resemblance without being literal variations.
Recurring DNA:
- A as gravitational center
- A/Bb semitone pressure
- D/C# pressure
- downward melodic gravity
- E7(b9)-type harmonic pull
- beautiful melodies repeatedly destabilized underneath
- recurring shapes are transformed, hidden, inverted, or reharmonized

The goal was NOT to clone any one influence. The record draws broadly from
dark industrial rock, grotesque cabaret, theatrical metal, dramatic musical
storytelling, and through-composed leitmotif technique.

TRACKLIST

01. ASHES UNDER GLASS
    Overture / psychological prologue.
    Dark chamber-orchestral art piece.
    Establishes the emotional language of the album.

02. A GOOD MAN'S PRAYER
    6/8 confessional ballad.
    The protagonist genuinely believes he is trying to be good.
    Piano, strings, organ. Vulnerable rather than heavy.

03. TEETH IN THE CHOIR
    Grotesque cabaret / industrial stomp.
    The outside world becomes mocking and carnivalesque.
    Crooked piano, clarinet, distorted guitar.

04. STATIC SAINT
    Cold industrial pulse.
    Identity starts becoming mechanical and detached.
    Minimal synth cell, rigid bass, prepared-piano clusters.

05. THE TRIAL OF SAINT NOBODY
    Theatrical courtroom waltz.
    Accusation, defense, witnesses, verdict.
    The song sounds outwardly theatrical, but the court is increasingly internal.

06. WHITE ROOM, RED HANDS
    5/4 intimate art-rock/chamber interlude.
    A suspended psychological room between acts.
    Sparse, disoriented, personal.

07. WHICH ONE OF US IS SPEAKING?
    Internal duet.
    Two melodic identities finally answer each other directly.
    They overlap, fight for register, and eventually land in a false unison.

08. MERCY MACHINE
    Violent thrash-industrial eruption.
    The darker self gets physical control.
    Fastest and most aggressive song on the record.

09. THE MIRROR EATS FIRST
    Bleak 6/8 lament.
    Aftermath. The protagonist can no longer fully distinguish memory,
    guilt, and manipulation.

10. NO ONE LEAVES WHOLE
    Full theatrical finale.
    New anthem theme plus transformed shadows of the album's prior languages:
    prayer, trial, duet, industrial violence.
    The ending returns to fragility but leaves one poisoned upper note unresolved.

Approximate dramatic arc:
beauty -> judgment -> mockery -> detachment -> trial -> dissociation ->
confrontation -> possession -> grief -> unresolved integration

All MIDI files are multitrack and intended as composition/arrangement drafts,
not final production or orchestration.
