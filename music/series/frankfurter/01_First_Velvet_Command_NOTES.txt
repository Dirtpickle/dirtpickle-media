VELVET COMMAND - first Frank-N-Furter-centered writing test

Goal: original song built around theatrical swagger, not a copy of "Sweet Transvestite."
Tempo map: 116 BPM -> 122 at bar 65 -> 92 at bar 89 -> 122 at bar 97.
Key center: A minor with Bb / Phrygian color; cabaret bridge shifts through C minor.
Approx length: 3:30.

TRACKS
- Vocal Guide - intentionally sparse, baritone-friendly actor-singer melody
- Piano - cabaret stabs
- Bass - sleazy pulse becoming heavy eighths
- Guitar L/R - held back until dramatic payoff
- Organ - gothic bed
- Brass - theatrical punctuation
- Drums - rock/cabaret to heavy

SECTIONS
1-8     The Entrance
9-24    Verse 1: Charm
25-32   Pre: The Grin
33-40   Chorus: Declaration
41-56   Verse 2: Threat
57-64   Cabaret Bridge: Smile / Knife
65-72   Heavy Build: Mask Off
73-88   Final Chorus: Command the Room
89-96   Breakdown: Confession
97-104  Outro: Last Word

The lead vocal was kept simple on purpose. The intended performance arc is charm -> arrogance -> threat -> command.
