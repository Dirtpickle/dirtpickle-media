THE HOUSE BOWS - second Frank-N-Furter-centered composition test

This one is built around one recurring two-bar strut so the song has a stronger identity.
The vocal guide is deliberately actor-singer first: short declarations, silence, response, escalation.

KEY CENTER
E minor / E Phrygian color. F natural and Bb are used as theatrical poison notes.

TEMPO
112 BPM through the theatrical material
118 BPM when the full heavy band arrives at bar 73
84 BPM confession/drop at bar 93
118 BPM final return at bar 101

SCENE MAP
1-8     The Door Opens - instrumental strut
9-24    Verse I - Invitation
25-32   Pre - Come Closer / stop-time
33-40   Chorus - Declaration
41-48   Turnaround - The Room Answers
49-64   Verse II - Predator Smile
65-72   Theatrical Break - puppet groove
73-80   Transformation - heavy band finally enters
81-92   Final Chorus I - Command
93-100  Confession - everything drops
101-108 Final Chorus II - Crown / last word
109     Dead-stop ending

DESIGN RULES
- The main riff is intentionally predictable and playable.
- The vocal guide carries fewer notes than the accompaniment.
- Guitar is mostly withheld until the transformation, so heaviness changes the scene.
- Brass acts like an audience/ensemble answering the lead character.
- The final vocal language is a distilled version of the earlier melody rather than a new melody.
