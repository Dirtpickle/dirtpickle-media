THE CURTAIN BITES
Frank-N-Furter performance model / escalation study
Original composition - not a transcription or imitation of "Sweet Transvestite"

CORE RULE
This version is built so each major section makes the previous one feel smaller.
Escalation is created by tempo, subdivision, phrase density, harmonic pressure,
drum behavior, guitar role, and vocal urgency - not merely by adding instruments.

PERFORMANCE IDEA
- Early vocal guide: sparse, declarative, almost spoken.
- Middle: phrases arrive more often and rests shrink.
- "No More Dancing": vocal rhythm compresses and the band starts chasing it.
- False Collapse: exposed long notes, huge space, half-time drums.
- The Chase: rapid call/response; no return to the original comfort.
- Crown of Noise: highest density and highest register of the song.

ARRANGEMENT RULES
- Guitars are absent for the first third.
- Their first job is punctuation, not riffing.
- Chugs begin only once the scene turns openly hostile.
- The false collapse removes momentum on purpose.
- After the collapse, piano, bass, drums, guitar and vocal all change rhythmic language.
- Final section is not "same chorus louder"; it is a different machine built from the same motif.
