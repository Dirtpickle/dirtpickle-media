CLEAN HANDS, DIRTY HALO — GOTHIC DOOM VERSION

Length: about 5:08

This is an original gothic-doom/dark-romantic reworking of the song.
It does not reproduce a specific Type O Negative composition.

Core changes:
- much slower tempo and heavier low-end
- B minor center with C-natural / b2 tension
- sustained fuzz guitars instead of stop/start chaos
- prominent low bass
- church organ and choir pads
- chorused clean-guitar figures in the verses
- narrow, low vocal-guide register with a wider chorus lift
- brief faster section as pressure release
- long funeral bridge and decaying ending
