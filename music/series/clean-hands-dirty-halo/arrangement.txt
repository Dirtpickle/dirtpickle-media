CLEAN HANDS, DIRTY HALO — original theatrical avant-metal sketch

Length: about 3:27.

This is an original piece. It does not reproduce a Dog Fashion Disco composition.
Instead, it uses broad ingredients associated with theatrical/avant-metal:
cabaret piano, organ, brass/reed colors, abrupt stylistic cuts, odd meters,
chromatic harmony, heavy stop/start guitar, mock-grandiose strings and a dark theatrical arc.
