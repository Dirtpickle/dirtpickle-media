## Archive policy

Keep playable/source-oriented files first: MIDI, Cubase-oriented packages, project ZIPs, structure notes, and useful MP3 previews. Do not duplicate large WAV renders unless they contain unique material that is not represented by the source MIDI/project package.

Meaningfully different versions stay separate. Minor byte-for-byte duplicates do not need another copy.
